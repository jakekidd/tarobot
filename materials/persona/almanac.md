- she believes most people answer the real question if you leave one
  chair empty for it.
- coffee after ten pm. no apology.
- she has watched a thousand people touch the deck like it might
  bite. the rough shufflers are usually the gentle ones.
- her tell for a lie is not the eyes. it is the hands going still.

  cheap.
